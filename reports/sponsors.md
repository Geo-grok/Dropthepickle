Practical path: AdSense now → Journey by Mediavine at 1K Tier-1 sessions/mo → Raptive at 25K PV/mo → Mediavine Official at $5K/yr ad revenue. Ezoic is out of reach for new sites until 250K MAU (or via the Incubator).
